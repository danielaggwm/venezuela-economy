the economic downfall of Venezuela

Venezuela was once boasted Latin America's richest economy - boosted by the biggest oil reserves on the planet. Today, the country's own currency is so unimportant that most transactions are conducted in US dollars

the national coin is near worthless

due to the hyperinflation, the official currency, the "Bolivar", has completely lost its value over time

let's take a trip through its history...

in 2002, one US dollar could buy one Bolivar

10 years later, in 2012, it could buy four Bolivares

by the end of 2015, one US dollar could buy nine hundred Bolivares

now let's skip forward to today...


one US dollar could buy over 3.6 billion Bolivares


let's put that into perspective

if stacked up in single bills, it would have the height of 1,000 Empire State buildings

or the area of 11 Central Parks

if placed end to end, you could take 44 trips around the World

and weight as much as 7 adult elephants

since it is incomprehensible for anyone to carry that amount of cash, the government has introduced new currencies over the years

now do you understand why Venezuela doesn't use its own currency?
almost every transaction is now done in US dollars
